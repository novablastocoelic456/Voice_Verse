## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

**Eva doesn't hear me**
- Make sure your microphone is connected and working
- Check that you've allowed microphone access in your browser
- Restart the application

**Eva responds slowly**
- Close other programs that might be using your computer's resources
- Try restarting the application
- Check your internet connection

**No sound from Eva**
- Make sure your speakers or headphones are turned on
- Check that the volume is not muted
- Try a different browser
